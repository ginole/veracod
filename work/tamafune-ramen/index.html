01 · The broth 豚骨

It starts at 8 am.
You eat it at midnight.

Pork bones, water and a hard boil, for eighteen hours. Scroll through a day in the pot.

Hours on the flame00
  1. 00:00Bones blanched, scrubbed, back in clean water.
  2. 01:00Rolling boil, lid off. It never drops to a simmer.
  3. 05:00Collagen gives way. Fat starts to emulsify.
  4. 10:00The broth turns opaque, the colour of milky tea.
  5. 14:00Topped up, stirred, bones crushed against the pot.
  6. 18:00Strained. About ninety bowls tonight.

When the pot is empty we turn the lantern off, even if it's 11 pm.
