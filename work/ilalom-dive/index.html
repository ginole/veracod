18 m · Dive sites

Four sites we
know by heart.

01

Panagsama House Reef

Shore entry. Sloping reef with turtles, frogfish, and the sardine school in the shallows.

02

Pescador Island

Fifteen minutes by bangka. A sheer wall on all sides, soft corals, and a cave they call the Cathedral.

03

Tongo Point

Coral gardens on top, a wall below. Gentle current, good for a second dive of the day.

04

Kasai Wall

Big gorgonian fans and the deepest diving we offer. Advanced card and a deep briefing.

24 m · The Cathedral

At noon, the light falls
straight through the rock.

A chimney in Pescador's wall opens to the sky. Hang still in the middle and watch the beam move across the sand. Advanced divers only.
