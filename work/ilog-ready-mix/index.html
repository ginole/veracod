01 / Process

From the river
to your slab.

Ilog means river. Our sand and gravel come from the Pampanga basin, and everything after that is measured, printed and timed.

Stage 01Aggregate

Washed, graded, tested weekly.

River sand and gravel are washed of silt and clay, then screened into sizes. A sieve test every week keeps the grading inside spec.

¾" and ⅜" crushed gravel
Fine

Stage 02Batch

Weighed by computer, to the kilo.

Cement, water, sand, gravel and admixture are weighed by load cells, not shovels. Each batch prints a ticket you keep.

±1% cement, ±2% aggregate

Stage 03Drum

The clock starts at loading.

The drum keeps turning on the road. Every truck is tracked, and anything older than 90 minutes at your gate goes back. You don't pay for it.
