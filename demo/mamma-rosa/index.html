The story

From Sicily to Lawrence Avenue.

Rosa and Giacomo Anfuso brought their love of Sicily to the Okanagan in 1984. The pasta has been made in-house every day since — 100% durum semolina — along with the bread and the sauces.

In January 2026 the Anfuso family passed the kitchen to Chef Mansor Abrahim, who spent twenty-three years cooking in Italy, with his wife Aktra Banu and daughter Arisha running the front of house. Same recipes, same table.

“Candle light, classic songs and jovial laughter filled the air, Chianti sipped and fresh handmade pasta plated… Viva Italia!”

Jordan Bradley, on Facebook
