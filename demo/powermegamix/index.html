01 / Process

From the stockpile
to your pour

Four steps between a quotation and a finished slab. Two photographs are still missing, and they are marked where they go.

Needed from youAggregate yard photo

Sand and gravel stockpiles at either plant. A phone photo in daylight is enough.

Step 01Aggregate

Sand and gravel, graded

Coarse and fine aggregate are stockpiled separately and kept clean, so every batch starts from the same material.

Step 02Batch

Weighed at the plant

Cement, water, sand, gravel and admixture are weighed for each load to the class and slump your engineer specifies.

Step 03Drum

Timed to your pour

Transit mixers leave in sequence with your pour schedule, so the next truck arrives as the last one empties.

Needed from youPour on site photo

A boom or line pump placing concrete on a real job. This is the photo buyers look for first.

Step 04Place

Chute, line or boom

Direct discharge where the truck can reach, a line pump for tight lots, a boom pump for decks and columns.
