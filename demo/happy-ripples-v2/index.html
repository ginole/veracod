Room to breathe on the Lingayen Gulf.

Happy Ripples is on the shore at Mabilao, on the west side of the Lingayen Gulf. That is why the sun sets over the water here.

Two pools, a long lawn, a volleyball court, BBQ grills, billiards and a restaurant. Guests keep saying the same two things: the staff look after you, and there is space for everyone.
