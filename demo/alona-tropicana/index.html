The story

Space that's only yours.

Alona de Tropicana is a resort in Barangay Tawala, Panglao, Bohol - single-storey rooms along a garden, with the pool in front of them. "A different vibe of your hospitality experience," the resort writes - a place to feel at home, with space that is only for you and your privacy.

Every room has air-conditioning, a flat-screen television with cable, Wi-Fi, a safety deposit box, hot and cold shower, and complimentary water, tea and coffee. The one-bedroom Bungalow adds a living room with a sofa, a kitchen with a refrigerator and kitchenware, and a private balcony.
