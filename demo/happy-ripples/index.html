What it's like

Room to breathe, on the sunset side of the gulf.

Happy Ripples sits on the shoreline at Mabilao, San Fabian — the west side of the Lingayen Gulf, which is why the sun sets over the water here.

It is a big property: two pools, a long lawn, a volleyball court, BBQ grills, billiards and a restaurant. Guests keep saying the same two things — the staff look after you, and there is room to breathe.

When to come

The quiet weeks and the busy ones.

Pangasinan runs on the same calendar every year. Exact rates are confirmed when you enquire — tell us your dates and we come back with a number.

QuietestJune – October Rainy season. The gulf is calm, the resort is nearly yours, and this is when to come if you want the place to yourself.
RegularWeekdays, Nov – Feb Cool dry months. Weekdays are noticeably quieter than weekends all year round.
BusiestHoly Week · Christmas & New Year · summer weekends, Mar – May These weeks fill first, every year. Book early — especially the Capiz houses, there are only two.

Rates move with the season and between weekdays and weekends, which is why we quote per enquiry rather than print one number.
