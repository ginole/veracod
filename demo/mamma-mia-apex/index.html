The story

Pasta runs in the family.

Ciro Formisano, born and raised in Naples, opened Mamma Mia! in Apex in 2004 — his first restaurant in the United States, with over 45 years of cooking behind him.

In 2019 his daughter Lea came home from Italy with her husband Valerio and their children, Matteo and Chiara, and joined the business as co-owner. Same classic recipes, a few fresh ideas, and her father's habit of taking care of people.

“Home of the Original! Our Apex location is minutes away from downtown.”
