The story

Sundays at Nana's house.

Sundays at Nana's house were special days. The smell of "gravy" simmering on the stove greeted you at the door, and Nana would fly out of the kitchen to pinch your cheeks and lead you to a table full of food saying "mangia, mangia".

Since 2000, when chef/owner Corbett Monica and his wife Julie opened the doors to this neighborhood trattoria, Bella Monica has been an extension of Nana's table — a wine bar, an outdoor patio, an indoor dining room and a private dining room, with the sauce still made the way Nana would.
