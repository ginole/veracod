Good to know

Questions guests ask.

Where is Sunbloom?

On the beach in Brgy. Uacon, Candelaria, Zambales 2212, Philippines, in close proximity to Potipot Island.

Is the restaurant open if we’re not staying?

Yes. The restaurant is open to walk-in guests from 11am till 8pm. For a romantic dinner, ask about the nights our resident pianist plays.

How do I book?

Pick your room and your dates in the booking section above and pay the deposit, and the booking confirms itself. You can also message us on Facebook, call or text 0961 840 9916, or email sunbloomhotelandrestaurant@gmail.com.

How many people can stay in a room?

The Penthouse Room with Balcony is good for four guests maximum. The Premiere Room is for two guests.

How do I pay?

In this preview, payment is simulated and no money is charged. On the live site, you pay the deposit online when you book, and the rest at check-in.

Are these the real rates?

Not yet. The rates and the deposit on this page are samples, to show how booking works. Booking.com lists Sunbloom from ₱2,850 a night.
