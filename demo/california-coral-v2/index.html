A day at Alubihod

No schedule. Just the beach.

Morning

Coffee in your room

Complimentary coffee and bottled water for two are waiting in your room, and the resort is right on the beach.

Midday

Island hopping on the Seahorse

₱1,000 for the first hour and ₱800 for each hour after, for up to ten people. The captain knows the best places for the tide, but you decide where to go. Snorkel gear for rent when available.

Afternoon

The gardens and the bamboo hut

The gardens and the bamboo hut, with a table in the shade, are free for room guests. Three public restaurants on the beach, one of them next door, serve breakfast, lunch and dinner.

Evening

Evening on the beach

The BBQ area is free to use. Charcoal is sold at our sari-sari store, open every day, along with the usual food and drinks.
