Good to know

Questions guests ask.

How do I pay, and how much now?

You pay a deposit of ₱1,000 per room per night when you book, by card, GCash or Maya. It counts toward your stay. The rest is paid at check-in, in cash or by bank transfer.

What if I need to cancel?

The deposit isn't refunded if you cancel.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is after 2 PM. Check-out is before 12 noon.

How many people can stay in a room?

Garden and Hillview rooms take up to five people; the rate is for two, and each extra person is ₱400. An extra single mattress with bedding is ₱300. The Beachfront Balcony Room is for one or two adults only. Children who can walk on their own count as guests; infants who must be carried stay free.

Is there a restaurant?

Not yet, and we can't provide prepared food. Three public restaurants on the beach, one of them next door, serve breakfast, lunch and dinner. Our sari-sari store is open daily with the usual food and drinks. Food is allowed in the rooms, but no cooking in the rooms. You can cook in the BBQ area for free (charcoal is sold at the store); there's a small electricity charge if you use your own electric cooker, outside only.

Can we go island hopping?

Yes, on our 40-foot boat, the Seahorse: ₱1,000 for the first hour and ₱800 for each extra hour, for up to ten people. Snorkel gear can be rented when available - ask before boarding. Sign up at the lifeguard station on the beach.

Do you have discounts?

Seniors and PWDs who show ID and their Senior or PWD card get 20% off their share of the room rate. Only one discount per stay.

Can we bring pets?

Sorry, no pets.

We're only coming for the day. Can we use the hut?

The bamboo hut is free for room guests. When it isn't in use, day visitors can rent it - it isn't booked in advance, so ask Toy, the manager, when you arrive. It's usually ₱700–1,000, depending on how many people and for how long. There's a ₱50 entrance fee for people passing through to the beach.

Can we just walk in?

Yes - walk-ins are always welcome when we have a room free, at the same rates.
