Booking terms & cancellation policy

These terms apply to bookings made on this website.

Who you're booking with

California Coral Beach Resort, Sitio Alubihod, Brgy. Poblacion, Nueva Valencia, Guimaras, Philippines. Run by Lourdes and Michael Rainey. Email californiacoralbeachresort@gmail.com · text 0927 727 0145 · Facebook.

Rooms and rates

  • Garden Room: ₱2,800 per night for two people. Up to five people.
  • Hillview Room: ₱3,200 per night for two people. Up to five people.
  • Beachfront Balcony Room: ₱3,800 per night, for one or two adults only.
  • Each additional person is ₱400 per night. An extra single mattress with bedding is ₱300 per night.
  • Children who can walk on their own count as guests. Infants who must be carried stay free.
  • Rates are in Philippine pesos and are the same for walk-ins.

Deposit and payment

  • To book, you pay a deposit of ₱1,000 per room per night. It counts toward the total for your stay.
  • The deposit is paid online through PayMongo, by card, GCash, Maya, or directly from your bank account (Land Bank, BDO, BPI, UnionBank or Metrobank online banking). Your booking is confirmed once the deposit is paid, and you receive a confirmation by email.
  • While you are paying, the room is held for you for 30 minutes. If the deposit isn't paid in that time, the hold ends and the room becomes available again.
  • The balance is paid at check-in, in cash or by bank transfer.

Cancellation and refunds

  • The deposit is non-refundable and is forfeited if you cancel your reservation or don’t arrive.
  • The only exception: if the government stops travel to the island — for example, when the boats between Iloilo and Guimaras are stopped because of bad weather — we will either refund your deposit or let you move your booking to another available date.
  • To cancel a booking, message us on Facebook or email us with your booking reference.

Check-in and check-out

Check-in is after 2 PM. Check-out is before 12 noon.

Discounts

Seniors and PWDs who show identification and their Senior or PWD card receive a 20% discount on their portion of the room rate. Only one discount may be used per stay.

From time to time we may run a special offer. It applies only on the terms shown on this website when you book, and only to the room rate. The deposit stays the same.

House rules

  • Sorry, no pets.
  • Food is allowed in the rooms, but no cooking in the rooms. Guests may cook in the BBQ area at no charge. There is a minimal electricity charge for using your own electric cooker, outside only.
  • Room guests pay no entrance fee. People passing through the resort to the beach pay a ₱50 entrance fee.

Questions

Message us on Facebook Messenger or email californiacoralbeachresort@gmail.com. See also our privacy notice.
