Privacy notice

This notice explains what personal information we collect when you book on this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Who we are

California Coral Beach Resort, Sitio Alubihod, Brgy. Poblacion, Nueva Valencia, Guimaras, Philippines, run by Lourdes and Michael Rainey. Contact: californiacoralbeachresort@gmail.com · 0927 727 0145.

What we collect

  • When you book: your name, email address, the room, your dates and the number of guests.
  • When you pay: the payment is handled by PayMongo. Your card, GCash or Maya details go to PayMongo, not to us. We only keep a record that the deposit was paid and which payment method was used.
  • When you message us on Facebook: whatever you choose to send.
  • When you visit this website: basic visit statistics - an anonymous visitor number stored in your browser, which page sent you here, how long you stayed and how far you scrolled. We use this only to see how the site is used.
  • This website loads its fonts from Google Fonts and some code from jsDelivr, so those services receive your IP address when you visit.

Why we use it

  • To hold and confirm your booking, and to send you your confirmation.
  • To contact you about your stay.
  • To keep the records a business needs to keep.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

  • PayMongo, which processes the deposit payment.
  • The companies that host this website and its booking system, send our emails, and provide the fonts and code the site uses, only so that the site and the booking system work.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as we need them to run the resort and to meet our legal and tax obligations.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to object to how we use it, to get a copy of it, or to have it deleted when we no longer need to keep it. Email californiacoralbeachresort@gmail.com or message us on Facebook.

If you're not happy with how we've handled your information, you can also complain to the National Privacy Commission at privacy.gov.ph.
