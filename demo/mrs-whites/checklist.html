03 — Your domain (registered in YOUR name)

One rule above all: we will never ask for a password. Not for your domain, not for anything. Anyone who does is someone to hang up on.

A — You don't have a domain yet (most common):

  • Send us: your full name, email and mailing address.
  • We set it up at the registrar (Namecheap) under your name and your email. You'll get an email from Namecheap — click it and set your own password. The account is yours.
  • Cost: about $11/year, billed to you at cost on the final invoice — we make nothing on it. It renews to you every year; we remind you.
  • You then grant us edit access from inside your account (two clicks — we send a screenshot guide). You can revoke it any time.

B — You already own a domain (you can log in somewhere like GoDaddy or Namecheap):

  • Just tell us the domain and which company it's registered with. We reply with click-by-click steps for that exact company to share access without ever showing us your password (most registrars call it “Delegate Access” or “Share Access”).
  • Prefer to touch nothing? We can simply send you two DNS lines to copy-paste yourself. Five minutes, done.

C — Your domain came “included” with your current website company (Wix, Squarespace, an agency):

  • Tell us — we'll check who actually owns it. Often it's them, not you. If so we help you claim it into your own account first (transfers take about 5 days — we plan around it). This is exactly the lock-in we're getting you out of.

04 — What we remove before launch

  • The "concept page" banner and this proposal section — gone.
  • Every press photo — replaced with yours.
  • Anything you didn't confirm in 02.

05 — What stays exactly as it is

  • Your phone number as the main way to reach you.
