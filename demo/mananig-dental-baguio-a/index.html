What we do

From a routine check-up to a full smile makeover. Message us to ask which treatment is right for you.

Check-ups and cleaning

Routine check-up, cleaning, tooth-coloured fillings, and fluoride and sealant for kids.

Orthodontics

Traditional metal braces, ceramic or invisible braces and self-adjusting braces.

Cosmetic dentistry

Smile makeovers: gap closure, 30-minute whitening, porcelain and composite veneers.

Zirconia and EMAX all-porcelain crowns, bridges and dentures.

Implants

Dental implants to replace missing teeth.

Extraction, wisdom tooth surgery and root canal treatment.
